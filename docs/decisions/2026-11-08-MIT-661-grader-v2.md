# Eval grader v2: words from a word's start, no version-only causes, versioned results
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-08

Context: The eval grader (`tools/eval/runner.py` `grade_proposal`, decision 0023) matched
`cause_words` and `symptom_words` as case-insensitive substrings of the proposal. Short words
matched in the middle of unrelated ones: "utc" in "outcomes", "view" in "reviews", "ops" in
"drops", "9.2" in "9.21", "polars 2" in "polars 2026". A proposal could also get
`cause_right` from a version alone ("broke in pytest 9.2"), which says when the cause arrived,
not what it is. Changing the grader changes what "good proposal" means, so results graded
before and after can't be pooled.

Decision:
1. **Words match from a word's start** (`runner.mentions`, for `cause_words` and
   `symptom_words`). After lowercasing, no `[a-z0-9_]` character may come just before the word.
   A word that ends in a digit must also end there, so "9.2" doesn't match "9.21" and
   "polars 2" matches "polars 2.0" but not "polars 2026". Any other word may run on, so stems
   and plurals written for substring matching keep working: "truncat" matches "truncated",
   "round" "rounding", "offset" "offsets", "overwr" "overwritten". Every false hit above starts
   mid-word, so the left boundary alone stops them. A word starting with punctuation (".prom")
   needs nothing before it. Words may hold spaces, dots or hyphens.
2. **Version words never make the cause right.** A cause word that is only a version (digits
   and dots, an optional leading `v`), maybe after the discovery's `library` or "python", is a
   version word: "9.2", "2.14", "0.11", "polars 2" (for polars), "python 3.14"
   (`runner.is_version_word`). Any other prefix makes it part of a cause, not a version's
   label: "pep 649", "rfc 3339". The data keeps version words; the grader skips them for
   `cause_right`. Tests check that every measured scenario and every GitHub task has at least
   one cause word that is not a version word, and that the skipped words are exactly 9.2, 2.14,
   0.0.45, 0.11, polars 2, 3.14, 3.0, 2.1 and 2.0.
3. **Every result records its grader.** `runner.GRADER_VERSION = 2`. Every new ledger line and
   per-session JSON carries `grader_version`; a result without it was graded by version 1.
4. **No figure crosses graders.** `agent_eval.py` `report`, `compare` and `effects` print a
   section per grader version, and `trials` a row per version. `compare` gives each version its
   own verdict, from that version's results alone, and says so when a condition holds more than
   one. The pre-registered verdict (the arms record) is read from one version's section.
5. **Proposals keep their text.** Each graded proposal stores its cause and fix, passed through
   the same scrubber and cap as the patch excerpt (`runner.excerpt`: secrets redacted, then cut
   at 4,000 characters), so a later grader can score it again. Older results, without them,
   still load.

Consequences: v2 rates of good proposals are not comparable with v1 rates; compare like with
like, or re-run under v2. v1 results can't be re-scored, since they kept no cause or fix. A
word that legitimately occurs as a prefix of a longer unrelated word ("end" in "endpoint")
still matches; a scenario list that hits this needs a longer word.
